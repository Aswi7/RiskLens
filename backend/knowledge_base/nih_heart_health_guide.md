# NIH National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute Heart Health Guide
Source: National Institutes of Health (NIH) / MedlinePlus
Topic: Heart Disease
URL: https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/heart-healthy-living

## Understanding Cardiovascular Disease & Heart Health
Cardiovascular disease refers to conditions affecting the heart and blood vessels, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and arrhythmias. Plaque buildup inside the coronary arteries restricts oxygen-rich blood flow to the heart muscle, significantly raising the risk of angina and myocardial infarction (heart attack).

## Major Cardiovascular Risk Factors
- High Blood Pressure (Hypertension): Systolic pressure >= 130 mmHg or diastolic >= 80 mmHg damages artery walls over time.
- High Blood Cholesterol: Elevated total cholesterol (>= 200 mg/dL) and high LDL cholesterol contribute to arterial plaque accumulation.
- Smoking and Tobacco Use: Nicotine and carbon monoxide damage vascular endothelium and accelerate atherosclerosis.
- Physical Inactivity & Obesity: Excess body mass increases cardiac workload and contributes to systemic arterial stiffness.
- Diabetes & Chronic Stress: Prolonged high blood sugar damages blood vessels and cardiac nerves.

## Heart-Healthy Dietary Protocols (DASH & Mediterranean Diets)
- Sodium Limitation: Limit daily sodium intake to less than 2,300 mg per day (ideally under 1,500 mg per day for individuals with hypertension).
- Healthy Fats: Replace saturated and trans fats with monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fats found in olive oil, avocados, chia seeds, and fatty fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines).
- Dietary Fiber: Consume soluble fiber (oat bran, barley, flaxseed, legumes) which actively binds to cholesterol in the digestive tract and assists in its elimination.
- Foods to Minimize: Processed snacks, high-sodium canned soups, fried foods, commercial bakery goods, and excessive alcohol consumption.

## Blood Pressure & Cholesterol Target Guidelines
- Optimal Blood Pressure: Systolic less than 120 mmHg AND diastolic less than 80 mmHg.
- Desirable Total Cholesterol: Less than 200 mg/dL.
- Desirable LDL Cholesterol: Less than 100 mg/dL (lower for individuals with existing heart disease).
- Desirable HDL Cholesterol: 50 mg/dL or higher for women, 40 mg/dL or higher for men.
- Exercise Goal: 150 minutes per week of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise plus stress-reduction practices (mindful breathing, adequate sleep).
